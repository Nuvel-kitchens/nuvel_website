Good to Know

Frequently Asked

How long does a project take?

Most kitchens take 8–14 weeks from signed design to installation; wardrobes and smaller joinery typically 4–8 weeks. We'll give you a firm schedule with your quote.

Do you work across the country?

We regularly work outside our home region for larger projects. Tell us where you're based in the quote form and we'll confirm.

Can I see materials before deciding?

Yes — visit our showroom and workshop, or we'll bring samples to your consultation.

What guarantee do you offer?

All of our work carries a workmanship guarantee, and the premium soft-close hardware we use is covered for life by the manufacturer.

Do you work with architects and builders?

Often. We're happy to work from architectural drawings and coordinate directly with your builder, electrician and plumber.
