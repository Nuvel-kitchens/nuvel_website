Who We Are

Cabinetmakers First

What began as a small joinery workshop has grown into a full design-and-build studio — but our hands are still on every piece. We design, manufacture and install every cupboard ourselves, so nothing is lost between the drawing and the finished room.

Over fifteen years and more than four hundred homes, we've learned that great cabinetry is equal parts architecture and furniture: it has to fit the building perfectly, and feel wonderful to live with.
